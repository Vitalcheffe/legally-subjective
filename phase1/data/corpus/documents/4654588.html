Proceeding pursuant to CPLR article 78 in the nature of mandamus to compel the respondent, Michael L. Pesce, a Justice of the Supreme Court, Kings County, to determine the petitioner's motion pursuant to CPL article 440 to vacate a judgment of conviction of the same court rendered May 13, 1987, in a criminal action entitled People v Shepherd , commenced in that court under Indictment No. 1985/86, and application by the petitioner for poor person relief.

ORDERED that the application for poor person relief is granted to the extent that the filing fee imposed by CPLR 8022(b) is waived, and the application is otherwise denied as academic; and it is further,

ADJUDGED that the petition is denied as academic and the proceeding is dismissed, without costs or disbursements.

The instant proceeding has been rendered academic in light of the determination of the subject motion in an order of the Supreme Court, Kings County, dated May 16, 2019, as amended May 31, 2019.
