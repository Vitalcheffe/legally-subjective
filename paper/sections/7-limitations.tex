\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Ground truth is a single coding.}
Our outcome variable---vote direction, liberal vs.\ conservative
--- is SCDB's editorial coding, produced and maintained by a small
team applying published conventions \citep{spaeth2025}. That coding
is the field's standard, but it is one team's reading of the cases:
directional coding of some opinions (especially mixed or narrow
ones) is contestable, and the database's own documentation flags
categories with known unreliability. We did not recode anything;
all weaknesses of the coding are inherited as measurement error
that attenuates every accuracy in this paper, the bar included.

\paragraph{A short, modern window.}
Nine terms is a small epoch by the standards of the forecasting
literature, and it is deliberately the hardest modern stretch:
benches changed twice, 5--4 rates are high, and the ideological
middle (Kennedy, then Roberts) moved. Consequences are concrete ---
the training window contains no votes at all for Barrett and
Jackson, so challengers receive no signal for them, and the B4
comparison excludes them by the $n \ge 20$ rule. The sealed-exam
results therefore separate \emph{known-justice forecasting} (justices
with training history, where the prior and the persona are both
defined) from \emph{new-justice forecasting} (Barrett, Jackson: the
prior is undefined by construction, the adapter is impossible, and
their persona degenerates, by pre-inscription, to the biographical
prompt alone). These are
different questions---a model that cannot know a new justice is not
the same failure as one that knows a justice and still misses---and
aggregating across that boundary without labeling it would blur
both. We consider the window a
feature (the numbers describe \emph{this} Court, not a historical
average) but it bounds external validity: nothing here transfers
automatically to other eras or other courts.

\paragraph{The transparent test is exploratory.}
The M3a comparison in Section~\ref{sec:protocol} runs on the
non-sealed evaluation window. Although every hyper-parameter was
selected by grouped cross-validation inside the training window and
the three model families were fixed in advance, the transparent
test was run once, on data we had already seen while computing
baselines; the multiple comparisons across three challengers are
not corrected. The sealed fifty existed precisely to absorb this
residual degrees-of-freedom problem, and the primary outcome was
scored under that seal exactly once.

\paragraph{The text conditions are now tested.}
The opinion-text corpus (M1.5) is complete: all 1{,}778 targeted
opinion texts were collected, normalized, and deduplicated to 793
distinct texts (the remainder are documented textual duplicates
across docket aliases), with a zero-leak audit of fourteen checks
committed and re-executed on every push. The memorization risk we
flagged before training was checked with the pre-committed audits
after the adapters were trained: the min-20\% log-probability probe
never flags an adapter (base-vs-adapter deltas stay below half a nat
on all seven, against a one-nat flag), and verbatim cloze recall is
zero for base and adapter alike on every sampled span. The
auto-coherence probe (Section~\ref{sec:results}) adds the sharpest
evidence from the opposite side: personas misvote on the very cases
whose texts they absorbed, so whatever the adapters learned, it was
not the votes. This paper's null now bounds the structured world and
the tested textual probe---not all textual representations (richer
models, full dossiers, other windows remain open), and not other
modalities.

\paragraph{Base-model pretraining contamination.}
The zero-shot and retrieval conditions read a base model whose
pretraining corpus is undisclosed and may contain reporting on the
sealed cases themselves. Thirty of the fifty sealed decisions date
from the training era (the \emph{interleaved} stratum), and their
outcomes were public before the base model's cutoff; the twenty
\emph{future} stratum decisions were decided largely after it. The
stratified analysis of Section~\ref{sec:results} is the design's
answer: on the interleaved stratum the zero-shot and persona
conditions make identical case-direction calls on every case, and on
the future stratum every condition, the re-fit ideological prior
included, lands at or near one half. The primary comparison
(persona versus zero-shot) conditions the same base model on the
same possibly-contaminated inputs in both arms, so contamination
cancels in the contrast even where it may inflate both arms
equally---but the \emph{absolute} case-level accuracies of the
language conditions on the interleaved stratum should not be read
as uncontaminated forecasting skill, and we do not read them that
way.

\paragraph{One rater of the pipeline.}
The corpus fusion, the docket matching, and the sealed selection
were implemented and audited by the engineering author alone, with
scripts, hashes, and logs---not by an institutional team; the
legal-research side of the project (framing, doctrinal reading,
jurisprudential interpretation) was carried by the second author
and does not re-audit the code.
The machinery is public and every claim regenerates from the
repository, but the code-level human audit trail is one person
deep, and we count that as a limitation of confidence rather than
of method.
