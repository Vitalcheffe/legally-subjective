\section{Reproducibility}\label{sec:repro}

Every CPU-side number in this paper regenerates from the public repository
\url{https://github.com/Vitalcheffe/legally-subjective} with three commands
and no credentials; the GPU-dependent stages are frozen as executed
artifacts, as described at the end of this section. The corpus rebuild chain is
\texttt{scripts/build\_corpus.py} (raw sources $\rightarrow$
frozen corpus with recomputed hashes); the baselines are
\texttt{scripts/m2\_baselines.py}; the M3a challengers, including
the grouped cross-validation, the McNemar tests, and the ablation,
are \texttt{scripts/m3a\_train.py}. The paper figures are produced
by \texttt{scripts/paper\_figures.py} and
\texttt{scripts/paper\_figures\_d1bis.py}. All randomness is seeded and
the seeds are printed in the outputs; re-running the M3a script
reproduces the numbers of Table~\ref{tab:m3a} exactly.

Provenance is structural, not narrative. The SHA-256 of every raw
source file is recorded at download; the frozen corpus recomputes
and checks them; the sealed fifty are identified by a
deterministic seeded draw whose seed derivation and resulting list
hash are both committed. A reader who wishes to verify that the
sealed list was not regenerated after training can do so from the
git history alone. The dataset rule, the known exclusions
(ghost docket groups, consolidated matters without SCDB matches),
and the coding caveats are documented in the repository's
\texttt{docs/} rather than hidden in prose.

The opinion-text corpus (M1.5) is complete: 1{,}778 collected texts,
normalized and deduplicated to 793 distinct documents with full
provenance, committed with a fourteen-check zero-leak audit that the
repository's continuous integration re-executes on every push.

The GPU-dependent stages are frozen as executed artifacts rather
than re-runnable scripts, and the freeze is the point: a sealed exam
that can be re-taken is not sealed. The persona training (M3b) ran
on free-tier GPU sessions under the pre-committed notebook
(\texttt{notebooks/m3b\_qlora\_personas.ipynb}, machinery audited in
\texttt{results/m3b\_*}), and its training report---including the
anti-memorization audits cited in
Section~\ref{sec:limitations}---is exported by the finalize session.
The sealed exam (M4) was scored once: predictions were generated by
a kernel pinned to the \texttt{m4-freeze} head, frozen under a
one-time seal whose lock records the head and the predictions' own
SHA-256 (\texttt{results/m4\_exam.lock},
\texttt{results/m4\_predictions.json}, commit f6145a5) before any
scoring was run, and the scoring
(\texttt{results/m4\_report.json}) is a deterministic function of
those predictions and the frozen corpus. The mechanistic probes E1
and E3 ran under a kernel frozen before execution (tag
\texttt{a1-freeze}); their outputs are committed under
\texttt{results/a1\_*.json}. Inter-run comparisons in this paper
follow the pre-registered rule of quoting each experiment's internal
numbers from its own single seeded run, never mixing runs in one
computation.
